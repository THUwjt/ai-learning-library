How to read the evidence

Measured submission: a published benchmark record with a specified workload and system. Paper-reported result: the authors’ experiment and baseline. Specification: advertised hardware capability. Derived estimate: a formula under stated assumptions. These answer different questions; comparisons in the guides preserve the distinction.

Start with the mechanism: what changes, what it costs, and which failure mode it addresses. Then inspect the result’s model, task, precision, hardware, batch, latency target, and baseline. A method can be valuable in one regime and counterproductive in another.

Figures include attributed excerpts from original papers, alongside explanatory redraws and analytical illustrations. Captions link to the source and archived PDF; clicking a paper image opens its local full-resolution crop. Calculators teach simplified cost and reliability models. This is a curated research review, not an exhaustive systematic review or an independent reproduction of the reported experiments. Figures are embedded or stored locally for offline reading. Keep the assets and talk_assets folders beside the guides for equation fonts, figures and archived PDFs; external source links require internet access.
